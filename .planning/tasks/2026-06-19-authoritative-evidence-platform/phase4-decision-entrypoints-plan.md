# Phase 4 Continuation Plan - Decision Entrypoints

Date: 2026-06-20
Level: CRITICAL
Plan ID: `PLAN-2026-06-20-DECISION-ENTRYPOINTS`
Status: COMPLETE

## Scope

- Add an executable recorder for human terminology mapping decisions.
- Add an executable submitter for human-authored PubMed claims through an approved worksheet.
- Preserve the machine-executes-only boundary: no automatic terminology selection and no automatic
  medical claim generation.

## Boundary

- The recorder may only select a candidate already present in the reviewed mapping request.
- The recorder may also persist an explicit reject-all decision.
- A selected or rejected mapping decision is itself a new review-required artifact.
- The claim submitter may only use locators from an approved worksheet and the raw PubMed artifact
  referenced by that worksheet.
- Claim text, stance, and evidence assessment still come from a human reviewer.

## Failure Rules

- Unapproved mapping requests block mapping decisions.
- Unknown selected IDs block mapping decisions.
- Empty reviewer rationale blocks mapping decisions.
- Unapproved evidence worksheets block claim submission.
- Missing worksheet locators block claim submission.
- Existing claim-ledger rules still reject empty claims, unsupported stance, pseudo-GRADE fields,
  incomplete four-part assessment, wrong locator hashes, and tampered source artifacts.
